## Rulings (Claude's; each with its cost if wrong)

- **R1. `conversationStudioHref(userId, designId)` decides where `/design?id=` lands.** It lives in `src/lib/studio-entry.ts`. It reads the bench's own lanes and returns `laneStageHref` (the primary, else the newest result), else `/studio`. The bench and the redirect cannot disagree about closed conversations or hidden results.
- **R2. A sessionless `/design?id=` goes to `/sign-in?next=/design?id=<id>`.**
- **R3. `id` wins over `prompt`.**
- **R4. Start from this image and Open conversation go to `focusHref` on the page's own image.**
- **R5. A lane with no results shows its title as text.**
- **R6. `/preview` fallbacks:**
  - no conversation id: stays `/design`, the doorway;
  - a non-owner with no front: goes to `/studio`;
  - an owner with no front and no primary: goes where `/design?id=` goes. Review finding 1 changed this case from the plan's `/studio`.
- **R7. `/design` loses `maxDuration`.** The explanation now lives on `src/app/studio/page.tsx`.
- **R8. The lightbox moves to `src/app/d/[imageId]/`,** keeping only what the image detail page uses.
- **R9. Only `getDesignThread`, `getDesignChat` and `getDesignGallery` leave `actions.ts`.** These now have no product caller and carry a comment naming the Ask-removal follow-up: `sendChatMessage`, `uploadReferenceImage` (that PR decides), `selectImage`, `deleteDesignImage` and `getDesignJobs`.
