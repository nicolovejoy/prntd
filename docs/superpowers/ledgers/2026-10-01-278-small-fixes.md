## Important

### I1. Image detail page: the phone sticky bar now covers ~90–110px of the page bottom

- Where: `src/app/d/[imageId]/page.tsx:105` (`pb-28`), `src/app/d/[imageId]/buy-panel.tsx:321-331, 351, 365, 579-587`.
- What: the expanded panel's phone sticky bar (`fixed bottom-0`) holds the whole `cta` block. Task 1.6 adds a 44px Cancel row plus a 6px `space-y-1.5` gap. The page's bottom padding is `pb-28` (112px) and was not changed. The plan's Step 3 told the implementer to raise the padding on `/preview` for exactly this reason; the same reasoning applies to the image detail page and was not applied there.
- Arithmetic (signed in, cart on, no size yet): `pt-3` 12 + "Choose a size" 20 + 6 + Order 48 (`py-3` + 24px line + 1px borders ≈ 50) + 6 + Add to cart 48 + 6 + Cancel 44 + bottom `max(12px, safe-area)` 12 = ~202px, ~224px on an iPhone with a 34px home-indicator inset. Before the branch it was ~152/174px. With 112px of padding, the last ~90px (no inset) to ~112px (inset) of page content can never scroll clear of the bar. The shortfall pre-dates this branch (~40px since the cart button joined the bar); this branch more than doubles it.
- Scenario: a signed-in visitor on a 390px phone opens a published image, taps Order, scrolls to the bottom. The last element in flow is `startAction` ("New design from this image"); for the owner, `OwnerActions` and the `ConversationImages` strip follow. The bottom ~90px of that sits under the bar and cannot be tapped.
- Checked: `page.tsx` main padding; `buy-panel.tsx` sticky block and `cta`; `button.tsx` sizes/variants (lg = `py-3 text-base`, bordered); root `layout.tsx` has no footer adding space; `git log -S pb-28` (padding unchanged since c153e13). Not rendered in a browser.
- Suggested fix: raise the image detail page's phone bottom padding to clear the tallest bar (e.g. `pb-60`, matching `/preview`), or make it conditional on `expanded` via BuyHero. Re-check at 390px with and without the safe-area inset.

### I2. `/preview` on a phone: two identical "Cancel" links with opposite scope while the source picker is open

- Where: `src/app/preview/page.tsx:1017-1023` (the picker's Cancel button) and `:944-951, 1336` (the new sticky-bar Cancel link).
- What: with the Front/Back source picker open, the screen shows two controls with the same name and the same style (`text-sm underline text-text-muted`, `min-h-11`). The picker's Cancel closes the picker. The sticky bar's Cancel, always visible at the bottom, leaves `/preview` for `/design?id=…`. The picker's own comment says its Cancel is "the only way out of a picker opened by mistake", so a buyer who wants out of the picker is likely to reach for the bottom one, which is always in view.
- Scenario: a phone buyer taps Change on the Front row and the picker replaces the hero. They decide not to change it and tap the Cancel they can see at the bottom of the screen. They land on `/design`, off the order page, and must use browser Back to get the size, colour and back picks they had. A screen-reader user hears two "Cancel" controls with no way to tell them apart (one a button, one a link).
- Checked: both render paths in `page.tsx`; `showSourcePicker` gating; the sticky bar is unconditional on mobile. The plan noticed the collision only as a test-query concern (Step 1). Not rendered.
- Suggested fix (owner's call, since he confirmed placement): hide the sticky Cancel while `showSourcePicker` is true, or rename the picker's control (e.g. "Close picker"). Either one keeps task 1.6's placement as confirmed.

## Minor

### M1. Image detail panel: same label collision with the back picker's Cancel; collapse keeps the picker open

`buy-panel.tsx:499-504` (back picker "Cancel" closes the picker) vs `:323-331` (panel "Cancel" collapses the panel). On a phone, with the back picker open, the sticky-bar Cancel collapses the whole panel. `backPickerOpen` is not reset by `setExpanded(false)`, so re-expanding reopens the picker. Lower harm than I2 because all state survives the collapse. Consider `setBackPickerOpen(false)` in the Cancel handler, and a distinct name for one of the two.

### M2. Image detail panel: Cancel drops keyboard focus and can leave the phone viewport far from the artwork

`buy-panel.tsx:326`. The focused Cancel button unmounts on collapse, and focus falls to `<body>`. Nothing moves focus to the `order-expand` button. On a phone, the user has usually scrolled into the picker stack; when the stack collapses, the browser clamps the scroll position and the hero artwork can sit above the fold. The task says "so the hero returns to the artwork", but the user may not see that happen. Consider focusing `order-expand` (and/or scrolling the hero into view) after collapse.

### M3. Image detail panel: Cancel during an in-flight Order or Add to cart hides the failure notice

`buy-panel.tsx:253-304, 352, 366`. Cancel is never disabled, as the spec says. If the buyer taps it while `buyPublishedDesign` or `addToCart` is in flight and the call then fails, `setNotice(...)` writes into the `cta`, which is no longer rendered. The buyer sees nothing, and the notice only appears on re-expand. Also, the `InlineNotice` now sits below Cancel instead of directly under the button that failed. Small and rare; noted for slice 2, which touches this block again.

### M4. `/preview` Cancel goes to the same URL as "Refine design", and away from where most buyers came from
