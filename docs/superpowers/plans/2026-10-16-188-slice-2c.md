## Rulings made in this plan (Claude's; Nico can reverse each, cost noted)

- **R1. One rule decides where `/design?id=` lands.** `conversationStudioHref(userId, designId)` (new, `src/lib/`) reads `getStudioLanesData(userId)`, finds the lane, and returns `laneStageHref(lane)`, else `BENCH_HREF`. So:
  - a conversation of the viewer's that is open and has a result goes to its stage, on its primary or newest result;
  - one with no result, a closed one, a foreign one or a missing id goes to the bench.

  This is the same rule the bench uses, so the two never disagree. Cost if wrong: one full lanes read per legacy link, which is what `/studio` itself does on every visit.
- **R2. A sessionless `/design?id=` goes to sign-in, then comes back.** It redirects to `/sign-in?next=/design?id=<id>`. Only a signed-in owner can be taken to the conversation, and minting a guest there would create a user who owns nothing. Cost if wrong: none; a guest whose cookie was cleared has lost the conversation either way.
- **R3. `?id=` wins over `?prompt=`,** as it does today.
- **R4. "Start from this image" (A7) and "Open conversation" (A8) open the stage on the image the visitor was looking at.** The href is `focusHref({ designId, imageId: <the page's image id> })`. If that image is not a cell of the conversation, the stage's existing fallback (one confirming poll, then the bench) applies. Cost if wrong: the stage opens on that image rather than the conversation's primary.
- **R5. A lane with no cells shows its title as plain text,** not a link (A6). It is already on the bench, and there is no stage for it (ruling 2).
- **R6. `/preview` redirect:**
  - no conversation id: stays `/design`, because the visitor may have no session and `/design` is the doorway;
  - a non-owner with no front: `/studio`;
  - the owner with no front and no primary: `/studio`.

  The last two always have a session.
- **R7. `/design` loses `maxDuration`.** No generation starts from it any more.
- **R8. The lightbox moves to `src/app/d/[imageId]/image-lightbox.tsx`,** its one remaining caller's folder, with its test. Props and branches that no remaining caller passes are removed with their tests: Delete, Publish, `guestPublishNext`, anything the thread alone used. List each one in the report.
- **R9. Only the three thread readers leave `actions.ts` in this slice.** Those are `getDesignThread`, `getDesignChat` and `getDesignGallery`.
  - Other actions whose only product caller was the deleted thread stay for now, each with a one-line comment naming the follow-up that removes it. Check `selectImage`, `deleteDesignImage`, `getDesignJobs` and `reopenConversation` against the code: list every export with its remaining product callers in the report, and add the comment only to those that have none.
  - The follow-up is the Ask removal PR.
  - Cost if wrong: a few server-action endpoints stay reachable for one more PR. Each one still checks auth, ownership and hidden state.
