## Tokens (PaperB, light only)

- Ground: warm off-white `oklch(0.97 0.008 80)`. Ink `#141311`. Muted/faint
  text as two steps toward the ground, not grey-on-black inverted.
- Borders: 1px ink (or ink at ~25% for hairlines). **No shadows anywhere.**
- Primary button: outlined ink, ink text (not filled). Links underlined.
  One mono label style (uppercase, tracking) for small labels/markers.
- Rose: ONE accent token (`--accent-rose`, pick a rose that passes AA on the
  ground for text and on white for the wordmark; document the value). Used
  for exactly two things in slice 1: the wordmark and the solid Generate
  button (the Studio composer's submit, the landing hero's Generate). Nothing
  else is rose. A fuller rose palette may come later as a token-only change —
  keep every colour behind a token so that swap is one file.
- `--positive`/`--negative` recoloured for a light ground (they are
  dark-theme `#4ade80`/`#f87171` today).
- There is no dark-mode mechanism to remove — only tokens. Do not add a
  `prefers-color-scheme` branch.
